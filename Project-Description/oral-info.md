# SOML 2026: Information on oral assessment

## Oral Assessment Date and Time

- 28 May, 29 May, 2026, location: Kwong Lee Dow Building (Building 263), Level 1, Room 101
- See Detailed Schedule below

## Oral Assessment Instructions

- The total mark for the Class Project is 25. Of these, 15 marks are determined by the oral assessment and 10 marks by the final report.
- Each group will give a 5-minute presentation, or a 10-minute presentation if you chose Project 3, followed by Q&A. The total assessment duration for each group is up to 15 minutes, or up to 20 minutes if you chose Project 3.
- **Your presentation should contain your complete results for the project.** The extra time after the oral assessment, until the due date of 12 June 2026, is for you to finalize your written project. You are not expected to produce additional results after the oral assessment.
- There are two types of questions in the Q&A phase:

  1. questions directly related to your project;
  2. questions on general knowledge of convex optimization and machine learning.

  The second type of question will mostly be conceptual and will not require calculation.
- If you chose Project 1 or Project 2, you can show **at most 6 slides**. If you chose Project 3, you can show **at most 10 slides**.
- For Projects 1 and 2, you can omit the problem formulation. Your slides should clearly show your methods and results. Specifically for Project 2, you only need to present your results in Part 6 (mini project), unless you have no results related to this part.
- For Project 3, you should clearly state your problem background, motivation and formulation before introducing your methods and results.
- The quality of the presentation will be considered in grading.
